The Problem

Retail Knife-Catching

    Forced Cascade Selling: Binance liquidation engines fire unceasing market orders, shredding through L2 bids and creating deep, violent price drops.
  • ❌
    Premature Knife Catching: Human traders and naive bots buy too early into the falling knife before selling volume has exhausted.

    Lagging Technical Indicators: Standard bots rely on 1m or 5m RSI/MACD candles. By the time they trigger, the mean-reversion bounce has already finished.
